Save states

Ten full-machine slots are stored on the router for each ROM. They include CPU, memory, video, audio and mapper state.

Keyboard bindings can be changed with Controls and are saved in this browser. Standard browser gamepads are supported. Click Start once to enable sound.

Keyboard controls

Bindings use physical key positions and are stored for this browser and router address. Click a binding and press a key. Backspace or Delete clears it; Escape cancels. Browser and operating-system shortcuts may not be available to games.
