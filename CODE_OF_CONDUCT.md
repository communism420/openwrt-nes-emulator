# Code of conduct

This project welcomes useful bug reports, careful technical disagreement, and
good jokes about routers doing jobs they were never meant to do.

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and specific;
- criticize ideas and code, not people;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and unwanted personal attention;
- respect privacy and never pressure anyone to share ROMs, credentials, logs,
  or personal information;
- disclose security issues privately.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, off-topic, illegal, or privacy-invasive
content and may restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct problems
through the repository's maintainer contact channel; do not expose sensitive
personal details in a public issue.
